Sustainability story

The catalogue explains a five-step model from crop-waste feedstock to responsible packing.

01

Feedstock

Rice husk, coffee husk, bamboo, and similar residue are sourced from farms or factories.

02

Biocomposites

The residues are blended with suitable binders to create usable material.

03

Products

Qualified partners mold best-in-class products from the material.

04

Bundles

Products can be grouped and customized for gifting, retail, and institution needs.

05

Responsible packing

Packing is designed to stay minimal and aligned with circular material choices.
